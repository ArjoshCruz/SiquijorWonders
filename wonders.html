Wonders

Siquijor is a paradise of natural beauty, rich traditions, and unforgettable experiences. From pristine beaches and majestic waterfalls to vibrant local flavors and charming island stays, every corner holds something special. Explore mystical caves, dive into turquoise waters, and savor the best of Siquijor’s culinary scene. Whether you seek adventure, relaxation, or cultural discovery, the island’s wonders promise a journey like no other.

Wonder Place

From hidden waterfalls and mystical caves to scenic peaks and vibrant marine sanctuaries, Siquijor is a paradise of natural beauty waiting to be explored.

1. Lagaan Falls - A hidden oasis surrounded by lush greenery, perfect for swimming, cliff jumping, and unwinding in natural pools.

Address: Siquijor Kinamandagan, Lazi, 6228 Siquijor

2. Sambulawan Underground River - A mystical underground river featuring stunning rock formations and an otherworldly atmosphere.

Address: 4JXQ+4H8, Lazi, Siquijor

3. Cambugahay Falls - One of Siquijor’s most famous attractions, offering tiered waterfalls, turquoise waters, and thrilling rope swings.

Address: 4JQG+XM3, Siquijor Circumferential Road, Lazi, Siquijor

4. Mt. Bandilaan - The highest peak in Siquijor, offering breathtaking panoramic views and a serene escape into nature.

Address: Siquijor

5. Lugnason Falls - A secluded waterfall with cool, crystal-clear waters, perfect for a refreshing dip in the heart of the jungle.

Address: 4GXM+CG6, San Juan, Siquijor

Wonder Activities

From hidden waterfalls and mystical caves to scenic peaks and vibrant marine sanctuaries, Siquijor offers unforgettable activities. Snorkel in crystal-clear waters, explore ancient trees, swim under cascading falls, and discover the island's mystical charm at every turn.

1. Snorkel at Tubod Marine Sanctuary - Dive into crystal-clear waters teeming with colorful corals and marine life.

Address: San Juan, Siquijor

2. Walk under the Old Enchanted Balete Tree - Step beneath this centuries-old tree, surrounded by mystery and a natural spring.

Address: Lazi, Siquijor

3. Swim at Cambugahay Falls - Enjoy refreshing turquoise pools and swing from vines like Tarzan.

Address: Lazi, Siquijor

4. Explore Cantabon Cave - Venture into an underground world of stalactites, tunnels, and hidden chambers.

Address: San Juan, Siquijor

5. Hike to Mt. Bandilaan’s Peak - Reach the island’s highest point for breathtaking panoramic views.

Address: Siquijor

Wonder Food

Beyond its natural wonders, Siquijor is a culinary paradise waiting to be discovered. Indulge in fresh seafood, local delicacies, and unique island flavors that reflect the island’s rich culture and vibrant traditions. From savory street food to unforgettable dining experiences, Siquijor’s food scene is as captivating as its landscapes.

1. Torta

Torta is a popular pastry in Siquijor, often enjoyed as a snack or dessert.

2. Tinalay or Pan Bisaya

Tinalay, also known as Pan Bisaya, is a traditional bread in Siquijor. You can also find this in some of the Visayan provinces.

3. Bukayo

Another famous delicacy not only in Siquijor but also in other provinces, Bukayo is a sweet treat made from young coconut strips cooked in caramelized sugar.

4. Sutukil

Sutukil stands for Sugba (grilled), Tula (stewed), and Kilaw (raw). This method of preparing seafood is a culinary tradition in Siquijor, as well as in other Visayan provinces.

5. Fruit Wine

Siquijor is known for its fruit wines made from local fruits such as duhat (Java plum), guyabano, mango, and pineapple.

Wonder Beaches

With powdery white sands, crystal-clear waters, and stunning coastal views, Siquijor’s beaches offer the perfect escape. Whether you're looking to relax by the shore, explore vibrant coral reefs, or enjoy the island’s tranquil beauty, its beaches are an unforgettable part of the adventure.

1. Paliton Beach - A serene and picturesque beach known for its powdery white sand and clear waters. Perfect for swimming and relaxing in a tranquil setting.

Location: San Juan, Siquijor

2. Salagdoong Beach - Famous for its cliff diving spots and crystal-clear waters, Salagdoong Beach is an exciting destination for adventure lovers and swimmers.

Location: Maria, Siquijor

3. Solangon Beach - A peaceful beach offering a calm atmosphere, ideal for sunbathing, swimming, and beach walks. Known for its gentle waves and scenic beauty.

Location: San Juan, Siquijor

4. Maria Bay - A hidden gem with calm waters and beautiful surroundings, Maria Bay offers a peaceful escape for those seeking a quiet beach experience.

Location: Maria, Siquijor

5. Cang-iping Beach - A secluded and serene beach, perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and enjoying the natural beauty of Siquijor’s coastline.

Location: Lazi, Siquijor
